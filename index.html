The History

The Pair That Was Never Meant to Be Parted

Written by the owner A record of sixty years

There are carpets that are made, and there are carpets that are commissioned. This one was commissioned — twice over, as a pair, on a single cartoon that the workshop never used again. Its twin has not moved in decades. This is the one that travelled.

Nain, and the Weavers Who Made It

Nain sits at the edge of the central Iranian desert, and until the 1930s it wove cloth, not carpets. When the market for its fine woollen cloaks collapsed, its weavers turned the same thread count to the loom. That is the whole reason a Nain looks the way it does: it was made by people who were already working at a scale finer than carpet weaving normally asks for.

Replace with what you know about this specific workshop, the knot count, the master weaver, the signature in the field if there is one, and the years it took.

Only two were woven to this design. The other has not been on the market in living memory.

Wool and Silk on Silk

The foundation — warp and weft both — is silk. That is the distinction that separates a fine Nain from a very fine one. A silk foundation lets the weaver pack knots more densely than a cotton foundation will allow, and it gives the finished carpet a drape and a weight that you can feel the moment a corner is lifted. The pile is wool, with silk worked through the outlines so that the drawing lifts and changes as you walk around it.

Before You Bid

Can I inspect the carpet in person?

Yes. The carpet is shown unrolled, by appointment, to registered bidders. Add the city and any notice period you need.

Is the provenance documented?

Answer plainly: what you hold in writing, what rests on family account, and what a buyer may independently verify. Say that the provenance file is released to registered bidders.

Has the carpet been restored or rewoven?

Answer in detail. Note any repiling, reweaving, ends and edges work, and when it was last professionally washed.

How is payment handled?

Bank transfer or a mutually agreed escrow service. Funds clear before the carpet is released for shipment.

How will it be shipped?

Rolled on a core, wrapped, crated and fully insured. Specialist fine-art shipping is arranged worldwide at the buyer's cost, with customs documentation prepared in advance.

Can I commission my own appraisal?

You are encouraged to. An independent appraiser of your choosing is welcome to examine the carpet before the close of bidding.
